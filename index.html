A Roman inn in the Borders

Locanda is Italian for inn, and that is the feel here: stone walls, wooden chairs, a bar lined with Italian wine, and every table dressed in red check.

The menu leans Roman, with carbonara, amatriciana and cacio e pepe, alongside fillet steaks, seafood and the house lasagne.
